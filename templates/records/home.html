System Capabilities

Intelligent Cadastral Protection Architecture

A multi-layered validation platform combining relational integrity, automated rule heuristics, and statutory revenue seals.

Cadastral Digitization

Converts physical title deeds into digitized cadastral plots with GPS centroid coordinates, precise square-meter area, and calculated acreage.

Automated Validation

Runs mathematical and heuristic checks evaluating survey number syntax, municipal subdivision ceilings, and circle rate sanity ratios in real time.

Litigation Tracking

Tracks active tribunal stay orders, encroachment notices, and title conflicts to prevent fraudulent secondary transactions and duplicate deeds.

Statutory Audits

Enables revenue officers to conduct physical and digital inspections, assign 0-100 health compliance scores, and compile annual fiscal reports.

Standard Cadastral Verification Workflow

1
Enrollment & Deed

Citizen is authenticated and title deed registered under cadastral survey numbers.

2
Spatial Demarcation

Cadastral plot area, GPS centroid, and classification (Residential/Agri) are assigned.

3
Rule Evaluation

Validation engine executes syntax, zoning threshold, and rate anomaly algorithms.

4
Officer Seal

Inspection officer reviews findings and seals parcel as Verified or Disputed.
